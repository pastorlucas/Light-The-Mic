What could I do with ₱500?

Start something

  • Buy candy or snacks and sell them at school.
  • Buy materials and make bracelets, keychains or crafts.
  • Buy ingredients and make something to sell.
  • Buy supplies for a small service you can offer.

Create something

  • Make art.
  • Record a song.
  • Create a short film.
  • Design something.
  • Build something useful.
  • Start a small content project.
  • Buy materials that help demonstrate a talent.

Help someone

  • Buy groceries for someone in need.
  • Feed someone who is hungry.
  • Buy school supplies for a child.
  • Help an elderly neighbor.
  • Identify a community need and creatively use ₱500 to help.

Invest in yourself

  • Buy materials to learn or practice a skill.
  • Purchase supplies for something you're already good at.
  • Use the money toward developing your gift.
Or surprise us.

These are only examples. Your idea doesn't have to look like anyone else's.
